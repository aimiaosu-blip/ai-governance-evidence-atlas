# Policy brief: a pilot for evidence interoperability

Independent portfolio analysis. Scope: three selected international texts, not a global regulatory survey.

## Decision proposed

Pilot a common evidence record for one low-risk administrative AI use case. Keep source requirements and review decisions visible rather than assigning a single compliance score.

## Shared vocabulary is a starting point

A shared transparency label can organize comparison, but evidence requirements and audiences still need explicit mapping.

Evidence: [O1](crosswalk.md), [U1](crosswalk.md), [G1](crosswalk.md). Analyst inference; review pending.

Consultation question: What information must each actor provide, to whom, and under what review process?

## Compare accountability mechanisms

A practical crosswalk should connect a named responsible actor with an oversight mechanism and evidence artifact.

Evidence: [O2](crosswalk.md), [U2](crosswalk.md), [U3](crosswalk.md). Analyst inference; review pending.

Consultation question: Which actor owns review, correction and escalation in the intended deployment context?

## Test interoperability on an example

A common documentation template could be piloted through consultation without asserting equivalence between frameworks.

Evidence: [O3](crosswalk.md), [G2](crosswalk.md), [G3](crosswalk.md). Analyst inference; review pending.

Consultation question: Which fields can be reused, and which require jurisdiction-specific interpretation?

## Practical next step

Use the consultation protocol to test the draft record with policy, technical and affected-community perspectives. Record dissent and missing evidence. Add national and regional law before making any jurisdiction-specific recommendation. No expert workshop or consensus finding is claimed.
