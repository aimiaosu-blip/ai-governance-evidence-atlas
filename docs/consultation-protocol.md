# Consultation protocol — proposed, not conducted

## Aim and participants

Test whether a shared evidence record can support comparison while retaining differences. Invite policy researchers, practitioners, civil society and affected-community representatives with varied geographic perspectives. Record selection limits, conflicts of interest and accessibility requirements. Seek consent before recording or attributing views.

## Proposed 45-minute agenda

1. Scope and terminology — 5 minutes.
2. Silent review of one AI use case and the crosswalk — 10 minutes.
3. Compare actor, evidence, review and escalation fields — 15 minutes.
4. Surface disagreement and missing perspectives — 10 minutes.
5. Confirm follow-up and attribution preferences — 5 minutes.

## Synthesis record

| Question | Proposed record |
|---|---|
| Claim or proposal | Verbatim note or clearly marked paraphrase |
| Evidence | Source ID and locator, or explicitly identified experience |
| Agreement | Who supported the specific wording; no inferred unanimity |
| Dissent | Alternative wording, rationale and unresolved assumptions |
| Scope | Actors, deployment context and geographic applicability |
| Follow-up | Owner, evidence request and review date |

No entries have been populated because no workshop took place. Do not fill this with invented expert quotations or relabel a prompt-based simulation as consultation evidence.

## Follow-up exercise

A later structured round could ask participants to revise their view after seeing anonymized reasons. Predefine recruitment and the treatment of missing responses. Report the distribution and dissent; repeated rounds alone do not establish expert consensus.
